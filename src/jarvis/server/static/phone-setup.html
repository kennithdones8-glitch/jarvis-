J.A.R.V.I.S.Phone setup
Back to JARVIS

About 5 minutes, at your Mac with your iPhone next to you. JARVIS stays private: your phone reaches it only through your own Tailscale network, never the open internet, and every approval from the phone needs Face ID.

1 · Tailscale on both

  1. On the Mac: the Tailscale icon in the menu bar shows Connected.
  2. On the iPhone: open the Tailscale app and switch it on, signed in to the same account as the Mac.

2 · Turn on phone access (Mac, Terminal)

  1. In Terminal, go to your JARVIS folder: cd ~/jarvis
  2. Make sure JARVIS runs in the background: uv run jarvis service status. If it isn't installed: uv run jarvis service install
  3. Turn on phone access: uv run jarvis remote enable. It prints an address ending in .ts.net: that's JARVIS on your tailnet, and nowhere else.

3 · Pair (Mac screen + iPhone camera)

  1. On the Mac dashboard: Status (the Systems panel) → Phone → Pair a phone. A QR code appears (it works once, for 10 minutes).
  2. On the iPhone: open the Camera, point it at the code, tap the link that appears.
  3. Safari opens JARVIS. Give the phone a name and confirm with Face ID. That creates a passkey that stays on your iPhone; there's no password to type or leak.

4 · Make it an app

  1. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  2. From now on open JARVIS from its Home Screen icon: full screen, like an app.
  3. In the app: Status → Notifications → Turn on → Allow. Notifications only ever say things like “JARVIS needs a decision”, never your content. (iPhone only allows this for Home Screen apps.)

5 · Check it works

  1. On the phone, Chat: today's briefing is at the top. Ask “hello”.
  2. On the Mac: uv run jarvis devices list shows your phone.

If something's off

  • The link from the QR code won't load: Tailscale on the iPhone is off, or signed in to another account.
  • “Not signed in”: pair again (step 3); the code may have expired.
  • No Notifications button: open JARVIS from the Home Screen icon, not from Safari.
  • Lost the phone: on the Mac, uv run jarvis devices list, then uv run jarvis devices revoke ID. To turn phone access off entirely: uv run jarvis remote disable.
