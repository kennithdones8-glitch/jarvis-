KERBSIDE GRAND PRIX
===================

A top-down, three-lap race against JARVIS and two other cars.

HOW TO PLAY
  Open index.html in a web browser (double-click it). No internet or install needed.

  Arrow keys or W A S D   drive
  Space (or Shift)        nitro
  P                       pause

  On an iPhone or iPad, use the on-screen pads: steer left/right, BRAKE, GAS and NOS (nitro).
  To play on a phone, copy the folder to it (for example with the Files app) and open index.html,
  or put the folder on any web server.

WHAT'S IN IT
  - Three tracks: Kerbside Circuit, Harbour Loop and Ridge Hairpins.
  - Easy, Medium and Hard: how fast JARVIS and the others drive. On Medium and Hard they use nitro too.
  - Nitro: the meter under your speed fills as you drive on the tarmac. Hold Space to use it.
  - Ghost car: a see-through replay of your best flying lap (lap 2 or 3) on each track. It appears
    from lap 2. Turn it on or off on the start screen.
  - Best lap, best race and wins are saved in this browser only.
