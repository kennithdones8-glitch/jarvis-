KERBSIDE GRAND PRIX
===================

A top-down racing game with a story career, set in the city of Port Kerbside.

HOW TO PLAY
  Open index.html in a web browser (double-click it). No internet or install needed.

  Arrow keys or W A S D   drive
  Space (or Shift)        nitro
  E (or Q)                shift up (drag races)
  P or Esc                pause (Esc in menus: back)

  On an iPhone or iPad, use the on-screen pads: steer left/right, NOS, BRAKE and GAS (and SHIFT in a
  drag race). To play on a phone, copy the folder to it (for example with the Files app) and open
  index.html, or put the folder on any web server. Some phone browsers won't run a page opened from Files.

WHAT'S IN IT
  Career        Five chapters and 18 story missions. You start with Nia Okafor's rebuilt Pacer and
                race your way from club night to the Kerbside Grand Prix. Rivals remember what
                happened on track (passes, close finishes, who hit whom), say so, and move between
                feud, rival, respect and ally, which changes how they race you. Your choices change
                later races, dialogue, your crew and which of five endings you get.
  Crew          Nia (mechanic), Otto, Pip, Lio, Priya and Dex join through missions and choices;
                each has a perk (cheaper repairs, more XP, more prize money, earlier events).
  World map     Four districts with home crews. Win there to take a district over; take one over
                and its crew wants a turf war (a 2 v 2 team race). A weekly special pays extra,
                rumours lead to secret events, and the rivals race each other between your races.
  Modes         Circuit, sprint, elimination, time trial with medals, drift, one-on-one rival races,
                drag (launch on the revs, shift near the top), touge (pull 66 m clear and it's over),
                endurance (tyres wear; stop in the pit box), delivery (checkpoints against the clock,
                mind the cargo) and team races. Some events are class-restricted.
  Police pursuit  Its own mode, apart from the Grand Prix. Get out of sight and stay there; units
                box you in, ram you from heat 3 and set roadblocks (always with a gap). The Ridge
                tunnel and the Old Town bridges hide you faster. Escape for the bounty; get busted
                and pay a fine (never more than half your money). Escape at a heat to unlock the next.
  Championships Four series (one in storms and changing weather) and two knockout tournaments.
                Rival rematches can carry a wager.
  Team          Name your team, pick its colours, sign up to three sponsor contracts, and fight the
                home crews for the team championship, season by season.
  New Game+     After the story: start again with your cars, money and crew; quicker rivals, bigger prizes.
  Quick race    The classic race against JARVIS, Bleu and Whitlock.
  Free play     Any track and mode with your own rules: laps, opponents and their car class, AI
                difficulty, nitro, collisions and damage on or off, time of day, weather and weather
                that changes during the race.
  Garage        28 cars in four classes, each with its own weight, turbo, grip and handling.
                Nine upgrades, tuning (gearing, downforce, differential, tyres and presets) with a
                live comparison and a test lap, paint, livery colours, decals, wheels, body kits,
                spoilers, finishes, tints, lights, exhausts and numbers. Damage can carry over
                between races (Settings) until you repair it.
  Replays       Watch any race again: four cameras, any car, slow motion, and a list of highlights.
  Photo mode    From the pause menu or a replay: move, zoom and turn the camera, pick a filter and
                a vignette, hide the cars, and save a PNG.
  Sound         All synthesised: an engine voice per car type, gear changes, turbo whistle and
                blow-off, crowd, rain, sirens, police radio, and music that follows the race.
  Conditions    Day, dawn, dusk and night (headlights and street lamps); dry, rain (less grip,
                spray, puddles) and fog, and weather that changes mid-race.
  Settings      Graphics quality (Auto adapts to your device), camera (Chase turns with your car,
                or Classic north-up), camera shake, reduced motion, damage (cosmetic or persistent),
                sound, music, volume, career difficulty, ghost car.

YOUR SAVE
  Progress is kept in this browser only (local storage). A backup copy is written with every save;
  if the save is ever damaged, the backup is loaded and the damaged copy is kept, never deleted.
  Settings > Export gives you the save as text (and a file) to keep or move to another browser;
  Import brings it back, after a confirmation. Records from earlier versions are carried over.
